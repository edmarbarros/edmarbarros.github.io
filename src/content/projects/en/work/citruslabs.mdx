## Problem

A clinical-trial recruitment platform was bottlenecked on slow customer onboarding (2 weeks), limited funnel visibility, and infrequent deploys that prolonged time-to-market for feature work.

## What I built

- Spearheaded a database-normalization initiative in MySQL, slashing customer onboarding time from 2 weeks to 1 day.
- Built automated patient-funnel tracking pipelines processing 1,000+ responses per trial.
- Designed and implemented CI/CD workflows with GitHub Actions on AWS.

## Outcomes

- 93% reduction in customer onboarding time (2 weeks → 1 day).
- Surfaced a 10% drop-off point in the funnel, informing recruitment-cost optimization.
- 70% reduction in time-to-market; higher deploy frequency.
