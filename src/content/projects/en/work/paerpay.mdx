## Problem

Paerpay's restaurant-focused mobile payment platform had to sustain sub-second latency during peak hours and integrate with diverse POS and payment-gateway systems — many of them SOAP-based — while giving partners timely visibility into transaction status.

## What I built

- Scaled backend services on Firebase and GCP to handle high-throughput payment processing at peak, maintaining sub-second transaction latency.
- Built a real-time transaction-monitoring pipeline on BigQuery giving partners instant visibility into payment statuses.
- Designed adapters for SOAP-based POS systems and REST payment gateways, enabling onboarding of major national restaurant chains.

## Outcomes

- Reduced support volume significantly by exposing transaction status to partners in real time.
- Onboarded national restaurant chains previously blocked by POS-integration complexity.
